# Library design: owner checklist

For the [library design plan](../docs/superpowers/plans/2026-11-12-library-design.md). Everything here was tried on a scratch folder over adb; these are the checks that need your eye, your pen, and your real Workbooks. Nothing below renames, moves or deletes unless it says so.

The first time you open each folder, its pictures are drawn again once (they now carry the page count and the writing mark).

## L1. Small folders opened out

1. Open a course.
   - **Expected:** each topic shows as its two PDFs side by side, headed with the topic's name, then "workbook" or "answer key". No topic folder to tap into.
2. Long-press one of them.
   - **Expected:** Rename, Move, Delete for the file, and "Folder: ‹topic›" with Open, Rename, Move, Delete for the folder.
3. Settings (⋯), "Open out small folders" off, Done.
   - **Expected:** folders as before. Turn it back on if you prefer it.

**Tell me:** are pairs easier than folders? Is three the right limit?

## L2. Progress and the writing mark

1. Open a workbook, go to a page in the middle, write a word, press ←.
   - **Expected:** its tile has a thin bar under the picture, filled about as far as you got, and a black corner at the picture's top right.
2. Look at a PDF you've never opened.
   - **Expected:** no bar and no corner.

**Tell me:** can you see the bar and the corner well enough? Too heavy, too faint?

## L3. A colour per course

1. Go to the library folder.
   - **Expected:** each of your seven courses has a stripe of its own colour beside its folder. Inside a course, the line under the top strip is that colour.

**Tell me:** can you tell all seven apart on the screen? If two look alike, say which.

## L4. Continue

1. Leave a document on a page you've written on. Go to the library folder.
   - **Expected:** the Continue row shows that page, with your writing, the file's name, and "page n of m". Tapping it opens the file there.

## L5. The list view

1. Tap the list button at the top (left of Refresh).
   - **Expected:** one refresh, and a list: name, and at the right "12 / 30" over a bar for files you've opened. A dot before the name of a file with writing.
2. Turn the tablet sideways.
   - **Expected:** two columns.
3. Tap the button again.
   - **Expected:** tiles again. The choice is kept when the app is closed and opened.

**Tell me:** which do you keep on?

## L6. Coming back

1. In a folder with many PDFs, scroll down, open one, press ←.
   - **Expected:** its tile is on screen and grey. Touch anywhere and the grey goes; the touch still does what it would.

## L7. An empty folder

1. Make a new folder and go into it.
   - **Expected:** "Nothing here yet", and where to copy PDFs to. Delete the folder afterwards if you like.

## L7b. A file copied in from the laptop

1. With a course on screen, copy a PDF from the laptop into one of its topic folders that holds two PDFs.
   - **Expected:** a third tile appears under that topic's name by itself, with its picture, within a few seconds.

## L8. Start in the last document

1. Settings (⋯), "Start in the last document" on. Close the app (swipe it away) and open it.
   - **Expected:** the document you were last in, at its page. ← gives the library, with its tile grey.
2. Press Home, then open the app again.
   - **Expected:** wherever you were, not the document again.

**Tell me:** do you keep it on?
