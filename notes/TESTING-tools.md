# Writing tools: owner checklist

About 15 minutes. Use the test copy, **Documents/E-paper Notes test/PWT test copy.pdf**. The app writes into real files, so don't use an original yet.

Open **Jot**, then tap the test copy in the list. Write a few strokes (pen and highlighter) and put a few on a second page first, so there's something to work on.

How the tools work now:
- **Pen button 2** erases: whole strokes (and snips) the trail touches go when the pen lifts. A thin grey trail may show while you drag.
- **Pen button 1** (hold it as the pen touches down) draws a thin grey loop. Strokes more than half inside get selected.
- With a selection: drag inside the box to move, drag a corner to resize (keeps proportions), tap **Copy** or **Delete** in the bar above the box, tap outside to let go. While you drag, a box and grey preview follow; the ink itself moves when the pen lifts.
- The strip also has **Eraser**, **Lasso** and **Snip** buttons, and **Undo** and **Redo** (grey when there's nothing to do).
- **Snip**: drag a rectangle over the page. The tool then goes back to what you had, and **Paste** appears. Tap Paste, then tap the page: the snip lands centred and selected. Paste stays, so you can paste again.
- **⋯** menu: **Rotate this page** and **Rotate all pages** (clockwise). Zoom resets to fit after a rotation.

- [ ] **T1. Erasing.** Hold button 2 and drag over a pen stroke, a highlighter stroke, and (after T5) a pasted snip. Do they go when the pen lifts? Does it erase only while the button is held, and write ink when it isn't?
- [ ] **T2. Lasso.** Hold button 1 and circle some strokes: is the loop thin and grey, and are the right things selected? Let go of the button and write: is the next stroke normal ink? Also try the strip's Lasso button.
- [ ] **T3. Move and resize.** Drag a selection by its middle, then by a corner. Does resizing feel right, and is the grey preview enough to aim with?
- [ ] **T4. Copy and paste.** Select, tap Copy, go to another page (and a rotated one, after T8), tap Paste, tap the page. Does it land centred and selected? Paste again: does it work more than once?
- [ ] **T5. Snip.** Tap Snip, drag a rectangle over part of a slide, tap Paste, tap the page. Is the pasted picture sharp enough? Did the tool go back after the snip?
- [ ] **T6. Delete.** Select something, tap Delete. Then Undo (does it come back?) and Redo (gone again?).
- [ ] **T7. Undo and redo through a mix.** Do some strokes, an erase, a move and a rotation, then Undo all the way back and Redo all the way forward. Are the buttons grey at the ends? If you undo something done on another page, does the view jump there?
- [ ] **T8. Rotation.** In ⋯ try **Rotate this page**, then **Rotate all pages**. Does the ink turn with the page, and does the view fit afterwards? Then open the file in Boox's own reader: rotated there too, with ink in place?
- [ ] **T9. Safety.** Erase or move something, then within 2 seconds swipe the app away from recent apps. Reopen: is the change there?
- [ ] **T10. Anything odd.** Grey trails left behind, flashing, slowness, buttons, messages.

## Things to watch

- Does the selection follow the pen smoothly while you drag? Fast-ink is paused then, and the preview uses ordinary e-ink updates, so it may lag.
- A stroke started within about 0.15 s after letting go of a selection may not draw. Does it happen in real use?
- After a quick erase and then writing straight away, the page refresh comes about half a second after the pen rests. Is that OK?
- Any grey trail (erase or lasso) that stays behind after the pen lifts.
- The strip in portrait and in landscape: all buttons visible and easy to hit?

Known for now: pasting between different documents and a partial (point) eraser are not part of this version.
