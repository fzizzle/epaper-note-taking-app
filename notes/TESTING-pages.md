# Dotted pages and fresh attempts: owner checklist

For the [pages plan](../docs/superpowers/plans/2026-11-12-pages.md). Adding, removing and fresh attempts were tried on the tablet over adb, on scratch copies. These are the checks that need your pen and your eye. **Use a copy of a workbook**, not the real one: adding a page changes the file.

## P1. Add a page and write on it

1. Open the copy. On any page, ⋯, "Add a dotted page after this one".
   - **Expected:** a moment's wait, then a dotted page, and the page count goes up by one. Undo is greyed out.
2. Write a few lines on it. Press ←, then open the file again.
   - **Expected:** the dotted page and your writing are there.
3. Go to the last page.
   - **Expected:** › has become a page with a plus. Tap it: a dotted page after the last.
4. Rotate a page (⋯), then add a page after it.
   - **Expected:** the new page is the same way up as the page before it.

## P2. The dots

Look at a dotted page with your usual Contrast and Dark text as black.

**Tell me:** are the dots too strong, too faint, or right? Too close together or too far apart? They are 5 mm apart and mid grey; both are one number to change.

## P3. Write, then add at once

1. Write a word, lift the pen, and tap + (or ⋯ and Add) as fast as you can. Do it a few times, on different pages.
   - **Expected:** every word stays on the page you wrote it on. Nothing appears on a neighbouring page, and nothing is missing after you leave and reopen.

This is the one thing I couldn't try: a stroke that the pen hands over just as a page is added. It's built to keep its page, and tested on the laptop, but only the real pen can show it.

## P4. Remove an added page

1. On a dotted page with nothing on it, ⋯, "Remove this added page".
   - **Expected:** it goes at once.
2. Add another, write on it, then ⋯, "Remove this added page".
   - **Expected:** it asks first. Cancel keeps it and the pen writes again. Remove takes the page and its writing.
3. On one of the PDF's own pages, open ⋯.
   - **Expected:** no "Remove" there.

## P5. Another viewer

1. Copy the file to the laptop and open it in any PDF viewer.
   - **Expected:** the dotted pages and your writing show.

## P6. Fresh attempt

1. In the library, long-press a workbook you've written on (a copy). "Fresh attempt".
   - **Expected:** "Made '… (attempt 2)'", and a new tile. Open it: no ink. If you'd added dotted pages, they are there, empty.
2. Open the original.
   - **Expected:** your writing is all still there.
3. Do it again on the original.
   - **Expected:** "(attempt 3)".

In a topic folder that already holds three PDFs, a fresh attempt makes a fourth, and that topic then shows as a folder again (small folders are only opened out up to three).

## P7. Anything odd?

A grey page shows for a moment while a page is added or removed. **Tell me** if it bothers you.
