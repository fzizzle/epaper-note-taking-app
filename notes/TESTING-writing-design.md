# Writing screen design: owner checklist

For the [writing screen design plan](../docs/superpowers/plans/2026-11-12-writing-screen-design.md). What a computer could drive was tried on the tablet (the progress line, Fit, zoom through page turns, the number pad, held arrows, the rotate offer). These are the checks that need your pen and your eye. Use the test copy.

## W1. The progress line

1. Open a long file and turn a few pages.
   - **Expected:** the line under the strip is black from the left as far as you are. Pages you've written on have a small tick under the line.
2. Write a word on a page with no tick, turn the page, turn back.
   - **Expected:** that page has a tick now.
3. Write normally for a minute.
   - **Expected:** no more flashes than before: the line never refreshes the screen by itself.

**Tell me:** is the line too heavy, or the ticks too small to see?

## W2. Fit

1. Pinch in.
   - **Expected:** the Fit button (four corner marks, left of ⋯) turns black.
2. Tap it.
   - **Expected:** the whole page, with one refresh. The pen writes at once and in the right place.

## W3. Zoom through page turns

1. Pinch in on the top half of a slide. Press › three times (or the volume button).
   - **Expected:** each page shows the same part, sharp. Write a word after a turn: it lands under the pen.
   - **Look closely:** does each turn come as one refresh, or as two (a blurry page, then the sharp one with a flash)? I couldn't count this from the tablet's log. If it's two and it bothers you, say so: the plain page-turn refresh can be used instead.
2. Drag the zoomed page up past its bottom edge.
   - **Expected:** the next page, at its top.
3. ⋯, "Keep zoom when turning" off. Pinch in, press ›.
   - **Expected:** the whole next page, as before.
4. Try step 1 with Trim margins on and with it off.

**Tell me:** is keeping the zoom right as the default?

## W4. The number pad

1. Tap the page number. Type a number. Go.
   - **Expected:** big buttons, no keyboard; you land on that page. ⌫ takes a digit back; a tap outside closes it.

## W5. Held arrows

1. Hold › for a second. Hold ‹ for a second.
   - **Expected:** the last page, then the first. A short tap still turns one page.

## W6. Duplicate

1. Lasso a word. Tap Duplicate on the bar (between Copy and Delete).
   - **Expected:** a copy a little down and right of the word, selected. Drag it somewhere.
2. Tap Undo.
   - **Expected:** the move is undone, then the copy.
3. Try it on a snip, and on a rotated page.

## W7. Tap to select

1. Choose the lasso (or hold button 1) and tap on a letter.
   - **Expected:** that stroke is selected, with its box and bar.
2. Tap on empty paper.
   - **Expected:** nothing is selected; the small grey dot goes.
3. Draw a loop round a word.
   - **Expected:** as before: everything inside is selected.

4. Zoom in a long way and do steps 1 to 3 again.
   - **Expected:** the same. A tap is anything under about 2 mm on the screen, at any zoom.
5. Highlight over a word you wrote, then tap the word.
   - **Expected:** the highlight is selected, not the word: the tap takes what was drawn last. Tell me if you'd rather it took the pen stroke.

**Tell me:** does a tap ever select when you meant to draw a small loop, or the other way round?

## W8. The rotate offer

1. Copy a landscape slide deck you've never opened onto the tablet and open it, tablet upright.
   - **Expected:** "These pages are sideways. Rotate all pages?", once. "Rotate all" turns every page, and Undo turns them back. Opening it again doesn't ask.
