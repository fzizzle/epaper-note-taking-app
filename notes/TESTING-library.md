# Library: owner checklist

About 15 minutes. Open **E-paper Notes**: it now opens the library, a grid of the folders and PDFs in one folder.

How the library works now:
- Folders come first, then PDFs, each in name order ("Lecture 2" before "Lecture 10"). About 5 tiles across upright, 7 sideways.
- A PDF tile shows its first page (with your ink) and its name. A folder tile shows its name and how many PDFs are directly inside.
- The top bar has the breadcrumb on the left (tap a part to go up), then **refresh**, **new folder** and **⋯** (Settings).
- **Long-press** a tile for **Rename**, **Move** or **Delete**.
- At the library folder a slim row "Continue: <last file>" sits above the grid.
- The folder on show follows changes on disk by itself, and also when you come back to the library.

**For L5 (new folder, rename, move, delete) work only on a copy or a scratch folder you make for it, never on original files. Delete really deletes the files.**

- [ ] **L1. Choose the library folder.** On the first launch (or in ⋯ then **Library folder**) the chooser opens at the tablet's storage, with Documents marked "suggested". Go to the folder that holds your notes and tap **Use this folder**. Does the library show it? Does the breadcrumb stop at that folder and never go above it?
- [ ] **L2. Browse.** Tap down through two or three folders. Go back up with a breadcrumb part, and with the Back button. Does Back inside a folder go up one level, and Back at the library folder leave the app? Is the order sensible?
- [ ] **L3. Thumbnails.** Is each tile showing the right page, with your ink on it? Open a big folder: how fast do the pictures fill in, and can you scroll while they do? Are names readable (two lines at most)?
- [ ] **L4. Open and leave.** Tap a PDF, write one stroke, tap ← . Is the library back in well under half a second? Does the tile of the file you just left update to the new picture soon after its save lands (a second or two)?
- [ ] **L5. Changes (on a copy only).** Make a scratch folder with **new folder**. Copy a PDF into it. Then long-press: **Rename** (try a name with `/`, or one already used: are they refused with a short message?), **Move** (into another folder; can a folder not be moved into itself?), **Delete** (does it ask first, and say what goes?). After a rename or move, open the file: is it on the same page, with your ink?
- [ ] **L6. Changes from outside.** With a folder on show, add, rename or replace a PDF from a laptop or a sync app. Does the grid update by itself within a few seconds? Does **refresh** work too?
- [ ] **L7. Continue.** At the library folder, is "Continue: <last file>" shown and does it open that file? Rename that file (L5, on a copy): does Continue follow it?
- [ ] **L8. Settings.** In ⋯ turn **Show thumbnails** off: do tiles become plain drawn pages at once? Turn it on: do the pictures come back? Change **Library folder** to another and then back.
- [ ] **L9. Anything odd.** Flicker, slowness, clipped names, buttons, messages.

Two questions for you:
- (a) When a file changes (say, after you write on it), its tile keeps the old picture until the new one is ready, instead of going grey for a moment. OK?
- (b) When you delete a folder, the question says how many PDFs go, and also how many other, non-PDF files in it will go too ("3 other files in it will go too"). Is that the right amount of warning?

## Also check (tools fixes)

A few fixes to writing came with the library.

- [ ] **T1. Older files.** Open a file you wrote on before today, ideally one with pen, highlighter and a turned page. Wait about 2 seconds. Does the ink look unchanged, and the right way up? Leave and open it again: still fine? (The app quietly saves older files once, the first time they're opened, to fix how their ink is stored.)
- [ ] **T2. Resize.** Select some writing and resize it. Does it stay the right way up after the pen lifts (no more turning upside down)?
- [ ] **T3. Tap off, then write.** With writing selected, tap away from it and write straight away. Does the stroke land where you wrote it, and stay?
- [ ] **T4. Home button.** With writing selected, press the tablet's home button, then come back to the app. Does the pen still move the selection? After you let go of it, does the pen write again?

## Things to watch

- The Boox home-screen icon should open the library (not an old start list).
- Dialogs (menu, rename, chooser, Settings) open without an animation. Any flash or fade?
- In a big folder the thumbnails fill in without blocking scrolling.
- Turn the tablet: 5 columns upright, 7 sideways. Are names clipped in either?

Known for now: if a file is changed from outside while it's open for writing, the conflict copies come in plan 5.
