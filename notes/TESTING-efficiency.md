# Efficiency plan: owner checklist

For the [efficiency plan](../docs/superpowers/plans/2026-11-12-efficiency.md). The numbers a computer could take are in the [results](../docs/superpowers/efficiency-results.md). These are the checks that need your pen and your eye. Use the test copy, or any file you don't mind marking.

## E1. A run of erases (decision 1)

1. Write five short words. Hold button 2 and erase them one after another, without long pauses.
   - **Expected:** the erased words stay on screen while you go, then all go in one refresh when you let the button go or lift the pen away. With the button held on and on, a refresh comes anyway about three seconds after the first erase, once the erase in hand is finished.
2. Erase one word alone and lift the pen away.
   - **Expected:** it goes about half a second later.
3. Erase a word and carry straight on writing.
   - **Expected:** the new writing is drawn as you write and is kept. The erased word goes once you pause.
4. Do the same with the eraser from the strip, not the button.
   - **Expected:** each erase is shown about half a second after it, or together if you erase faster than that.

**Tell me:** is one refresh at the end better than one per erase, or do you miss seeing each word go at once? Is three seconds too long?

## E2. Autosave after ten seconds (decision 2)

1. Write a few lines with pauses to think, then press ← at once.
2. Open the file again.
   - **Expected:** everything is there.
3. Write a line, then switch the tablet off at the button within five seconds. Switch on and look.
   - **Expected:** everything is there.

## E3. Page turns with Trim margins

1. In a workbook with Trim margins on, turn ten pages forward with ›, then a few back.
   - **Expected:** each page appears as quickly as before, already fitted, with no second jump.
2. Turn Trim margins off under ⋯ and turn a few pages.
   - **Expected:** as before.

## E4. Leaving a document

1. Open a long file, turn a dozen pages, press ←, and open another file straight away.
   - **Expected:** the library and the next file appear as quickly as before, with no grey page that stays.

## E5. A folder filling in (decision 3)

1. Close the app (swipe it away), open it, and go into a folder with many PDFs.
   - **Expected:** the pictures appear together, in one or two goes, not tile by tile.
2. Scroll down and back up.
   - **Expected:** pictures for the new rows appear as they come into view.

## E6. The memory card out

1. Close the app, take the SD card out, and open the app.
   - **Expected:** no crash: the note that the library folder is on the card, which isn't in.
2. Put the card back and tap the note.
   - **Expected:** the library, with its pictures there at once, not drawn again one by one.

## E7. Anything slower?

Use the app as you normally would for a session. Tell me about anything that feels slower or looks different from before.
