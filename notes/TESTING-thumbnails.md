# Lighter thumbnails: owner checklist

For the [lighter thumbnails plan](../docs/superpowers/plans/2026-11-13-lighter-thumbnails.md). The numbers are in the [results](../docs/superpowers/thumbnail-results.md). These are the checks that need your eye.

## T1. Folders open in one go

1. Open the app and stay in the library for a minute (the thumbnails of the whole library are drawn in that time, quietly).
2. Open each course, and a few topics.
   - **Expected:** each folder comes on with its pictures, in one refresh: no grey pages first, no tiles filling in a few at a time.
3. Leave a document and come back to its course.
   - **Expected:** the same, with the document's tile marked.

## T2. Writing isn't slower

1. Open the app and, straight away, open a document and write.
   - **Expected:** the pen is as quick as ever. (No thumbnail is drawn while a document is open.)

## T3. The pictures

1. Look at the tiles closely.
   - **Expected:** as sharp as before. They are now made at the size they're shown, 240 px wide instead of 300.

## T4. The gentler refresh (a trial)

The library now goes onto the panel with the same update you chose for page turns (REGAL), not the plain full-screen flash.

1. Open folders, go back, scroll a long folder.

**Tell me:** better, worse, or no difference? If worse, it goes back to how it was.
